# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and people of all backgrounds and experience levels.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or doxxing.
- Critique the work, not the person.

Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders. Report concerns by opening a private security advisory or contacting the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
