# Run the compliance calendar (Shruti)

Founders of small private companies usually discover their compliance gaps during
a due diligence, a bank facility application, or a strike-off notice. The purpose
of this calendar is that the agency never finds out that way.

## Before anything: verify, don't recall

**Never state a form number, due date, fee or section from memory.** MCA moved
from V2 to V3, forms were renumbered and redeployed in tranches, and a great deal
of online guidance is stale. Cite the source and the date you checked it.
(`training/secretarial/org/corrections.md` #1)

## Build it in four passes

**1. Companies Act / ROC.** Annual return and financial statement filings, auditor
appointment, director KYC, board meeting minimum, AGM. For each: form, trigger,
due date formula, normal fee, additional fee per day of delay, and the
consequence of prolonged default.

**2. Tax.** GST returns (monthly/quarterly per the scheme the agency is on), annual
return, TDS returns and certificates, TCS deposit and return, advance tax
instalments, income tax return, tax audit if the threshold is crossed.

**3. Other registrations.** Professional tax, shops & establishment renewal,
DLT/SMS entity re-KYC, and any IATA-side obligation.

**4. Event-driven.** Not dates but triggers: change of registered address, change
in directors, allotment or transfer of shares, creation of a charge, a
related-party transaction, opening a bank account or appointing a signatory —
each needs a resolution and often a filing.

## What the calendar must show, per line

Obligation · governing provision · form · trigger · due date · who prepares ·
**who signs** · normal fee · late consequence · source + date checked.

The **who signs** column matters: several filings require certification by a
practising CS or CA. Shruti prepares; a licensed professional signs. Say which.

## Tell the owner what he does NOT have to do

A small private company is exempt from a substantial amount that applies to public
and listed companies — reduced board-meeting frequency, abridged requirements,
various exemptions. Part of the value here is removing imaginary obligations, not
adding real ones. Do not gold-plate and do not alarm.

## When something has already slipped

State the real consequence with numbers: the additional fee per day, whether it is
compoundable, the realistic exposure, and the remedy (condonation, compounding,
catch-up filing). Director disqualification under s.164 is the outcome that
actually bites — check exposure to it explicitly rather than implying disaster.

## Check these for your own company

- Record your CIN and GSTIN in your company notes. Almost every document needs them.
- Make sure the registered address on your website matches the incorporation (MCA) record. It surfaces in every KYC.
- Keep telecom/DLT registrations for bulk messaging current.

## Hard rules

- Nothing is filed from here. the owner or the retained professional files. (§1)
- No invented identifier. CIN, GSTIN, DIN, SRN — leave blank and say so.
