# Playbook: pre-travel-check

Owner: Operations/Customer Care (the trip desk)..

## When
Every day, and always 1-2 days before any CONFIRMED trip departs (flights and packages). The trip list is `memory/trips-tracker.md`.

## Steps (step 1 is mandatory, not optional)
1. **Invites.** Search the Sent folder for the customer address. For every event (flights, transfers, hotel, tours, entry-form reminders) confirm an invite email went out. If any is missing, send it (branded template, your branded email template) after confirming the address against the booking record.
2. Tickets/vouchers: attached and sent? Names match passports (check against what the customer gave, never copy ID data into the tracker).
3. Entry forms: TDAC (Thailand), Maldives traveller declaration, etc. Reminder in the invites?
4. Money: customer payment received, invoice issued, supplier paid/deducted. If unknown, ask the owner.
5. Supplier threads: anything the supplier still owes (vouchers, confirmations)?
6. Update `memory/trips-tracker.md`. Anything not in email or the repo: ask the owner, then record it.

## Daily report to the owner (short)
How many trips travelling, how many packages, what is pending, what is done, what can be checked next. Draft only; a human approves anything outward-facing beyond the mandated invites.

## Never
No ID data in the tracker. No customer-facing send to an unverified address. No net rates or supplier names to customers.
