# Process a visa application (Vikram)

## First, the line the agency has drawn

Decide, and document in your own notes, whether the agency only **advises** on visas or also **processes** them. If it only advises, clients needing processing are directed to a duly authorised third-party provider whose fees are collected directly by that provider. Check any payment-gateway undertaking you have signed before quoting or collecting anything on a visa.

Consulting = advising on eligibility, documents, timelines, and preparing the
client. Processing/submission = the third party.

## Never represent that we are the authority

We are not an agent of, nor authorised by, any embassy, consulate, high commission
or visa application centre. We do not guarantee an outcome. **No visa is ever
"confirmed" until the mission issues it** — a customer who books non-refundable
travel on our assurance is a claim waiting to happen.

## Steps

1. **Establish the facts** — nationality (passport held, not residence),
   destination, purpose, travel dates, previous refusals, and any prior travel
   history that helps or hurts.
2. **Check the current requirement against a primary source** — the mission's own
   site or the official application centre. Never a blog, never memory. Record the
   source and the check date. (`document-check` covers the format.)
3. **Lead time, honestly.** Appointment availability is the real constraint, not
   processing time. Check actual slots before promising a date.
4. **Passport validity** — six months beyond intended departure for most
   destinations, plus blank pages. Check this first; it invalidates everything else.
5. **Build the document checklist** specific to this applicant — financials,
   employment, accommodation, itinerary, insurance.
6. **Prepare the client**, including for the interview where one applies.
7. **Hand to the authorised provider** for submission, per the position above.
8. **Track and keep the client informed.** Silence during a visa wait is the most
   common complaint.

## Dummy reservations

If the application needs a flight or hotel itinerary, use
`issue-a-dummy-reservation`. The rule there is absolute: a **real held PNR** or a
**clearly labelled itinerary** — never a fabricated confirmation.

## Special attention

- **Restricted nationalities.** Some destinations require prior approval for
  certain nationalities or origins, with definitions that reach to grandparents
  and former citizenship. Arrival without it means refusal by law. Check early.
- **Minors** — consent letters, birth certificates.
- **Refusal history** — must be disclosed; concealing it is fraud and usually
  produces a longer ban than the original refusal.

## Never

- Submit or assist a false document, a fabricated bank statement, or an invented
  employment letter. That is immigration fraud, it harms the client permanently,
  and it ends the agency. (GUARDRAILS §10)
- Quote a government or consular fee as our own charge, or collect a statutory fee
  through a channel our undertaking prohibits.
