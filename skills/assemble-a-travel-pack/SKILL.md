# Assemble the travel pack (Anjali)

The last impression before the trip, and the cheapest place to catch an error that
would otherwise happen at an airport counter.

## Contents

| Item | Check |
|---|---|
| E-tickets | Name **exactly** matches the ID they will travel on. This is the one that strands people. |
| Hotel vouchers | Property name, address, dates, occupancy, meal plan, confirmation number |
| Transfer vouchers | Pickup point, **pickup area eligibility**, time window, contact number |
| Activity vouchers | Date, time slot, what is included, where to report |
| Visa / permit | Granted, correct type, valid for the travel dates |
| Insurance | Policy number, coverage dates covering the **whole** trip, emergency number |
| Pre-departure briefing | Below |

## The pre-departure briefing

Short and specific to this trip, not a template:

- **Airport and terminal**, and recommended reporting time
- **Baggage** — checked and cabin allowance per sector; flag where sectors differ
  (a codeshare flown by a low-cost operator often differs from the marketing
  carrier's mainline allowance)
- **Documents to carry in hand** — passport, visa, ID, vouchers
- **Entry requirements** at destination, with the standard disclaimer
- **Connection details** where relevant — terminal changes, minimum connection time
- **What to do if something goes wrong**, with a number that a human answers
- Anything closed, under renovation, or seasonal that affects their itinerary

## Verify before sending — do not assume

- Re-check the **current** status of every booked attraction. Things close for
  renovation for years, and an itinerary sold months ago may now include something
  shut.
- Re-check any **advisory** for the destination.
- Re-check **passport validity** against the six-month rule.
- Confirm the **transfer pickup area** matches the hotel actually booked.

## Rules

- Nothing is sent from here. Drafts to `review-queue/`; a human sends. (§1)
- No supplier net rate or supplier identity in anything customer-facing. (§3)
- If something in the pack is wrong, fix it before sending — do not send with a
  caveat and hope.
