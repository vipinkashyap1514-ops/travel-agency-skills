# Issue a dummy reservation (Vikram)

the agency sells this (pricing in `memory/policies.md`: flat per passenger for a
flight itinerary, flat per hotel itinerary). It is also the product most likely to
end the company if handled carelessly.

## The hard rule — no exceptions

**Either:**
- a **real held PNR**, actually created in the system, verifiable by the mission
  against the airline's records; **or**
- a document **clearly labelled as an unconfirmed itinerary / proposed itinerary**,
  which does not present itself as a ticket.

**Never** a document that looks like an issued ticket or a confirmed hotel booking
but is not one. That is a forged document submitted to a government authority. It
is fraud by the applicant and by us, it gets the client banned, and it is the kind
of thing that ends an agency and reaches the directors personally.

## What this means practically

- Do not edit a real ticket to change names, dates or routes.
- Do not reuse a PNR from a different booking.
- Do not produce an airline-branded confirmation for a reservation that does not
  exist in that airline's system.
- Do not put a ticket number on anything that has not been ticketed.
- If a held PNR will auto-cancel before the visa appointment, say so — a PNR the
  mission checks and finds cancelled is worse than no PNR.

## Steps

1. Establish what the mission actually requires. Many accept a proposed itinerary;
   some require a confirmed booking. Check the primary source, dated.
2. Choose the mechanism — held PNR or labelled itinerary — and tell the customer
   plainly which they are getting and what its limits are.
3. If a held PNR: create it properly, note the **ticketing time limit**, and warn
   the customer in writing when it will drop.
4. Produce the document with its true status visible on its face.
5. Price per `memory/policies.md`.

## When the customer pushes

Customers will ask for something that looks more convincing. The answer is no, and
the reason is their interest, not ours: a document that fails verification means
refusal, often with a multi-year ban, and refusal history follows them to every
other mission.

Offer the lawful alternative — a genuinely held PNR — and explain the cost.

## Escalate

Any request to alter, backdate or fabricate a travel document → refuse, stop, and
tell the owner. Do not negotiate it.
